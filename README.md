# NoiseBolt

**The soundboard for gamers and streamers.** Play sound effects and music to your friends while you talk, in Discord, TeamSpeak, in-game voice chat, OBS or calls. They hear your voice and your sounds together, and you hear them too.

**[Download the latest NoiseBolt.exe](../../releases/latest)**: free, for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Run it and the NoiseBolt installer opens.

More about NoiseBolt, NoiseBolt Pro and your account: **[noisebolt.com](https://noisebolt.com)**

## What it does

- **Separate volume for each app:** send sounds to Discord, OBS and your game at once, each with its own volume and a one-click mute.
- **Your mic, cleaned:** noise removal, a smart gate and a compressor run on your voice inside NoiseBolt.
- **Every clip at the same loudness:** each sound is measured once and levelled, and you can still adjust any sound by hand.
- **Trim to the good bit:** set where each sound starts and ends. Stopping a sound fades it out, so it never clicks.
- **Global hotkeys** that work in any game, plus free **Stream Deck** and **Touch Portal** plugins.
- **In-game overlay:** press Ctrl+Alt+O for a small card over your game showing what's playing and your hotkey sounds. Clicks pass straight through it.
- **Record your own clips** from whatever your PC is playing.
- **Sixteen colour presets.**
- **Automatic updates:** NoiseBolt checks this page for new versions and updates in one click.

**NoiseBolt Pro** adds your phone as a hotkey remote, Twitch alerts that play sounds, cloud sync, game profiles, voice effects, text-to-speech pads, music ducking, and random pads and sequences. See [noisebolt.com](https://noisebolt.com).

## Getting started

1. **Install VB-Cable (one time).** NoiseBolt sends your sounds and voice to your chat app through this free "virtual cable". Download it from [vb-audio.com/Cable](https://vb-audio.com/Cable/), unzip it, right-click **VBCABLE_Setup_x64.exe**, choose **Run as administrator** and click **Install Driver**. NoiseBolt's first-run setup checks for it and takes you there if it's missing.
2. **Install NoiseBolt.** Download **NoiseBolt.exe** from [Releases](../../releases/latest) and double-click it. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Then click **Install NoiseBolt**.
3. **Follow the short setup.** It asks which chat app you use and shows exactly what to set in it.
4. **Add your sounds.** Drop files, folders or a .zip onto the window. MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A, AAC, WMA and AIFF all work.

To remove it later: **Settings › Apps › Installed apps › NoiseBolt › Uninstall**. Your sound files are never touched.
